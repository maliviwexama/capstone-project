# General Feature Updates

This update addresses the additional group feedback supplied on 23 September 2026.

1. Duplicate account prevention
   - Email addresses are normalized and checked before registration/admin account creation.
   - Student numbers and Staff IDs are also checked for duplicates.

2. UFH Protection Services
   - Emergency page includes direct call links for UFH Protection Services.

3. GBV / Sexual Violence
   - Emergency page includes UFH GBV support and national GBV support call links.

4. Feedback without a rating
   - Rating is now optional; a student can submit a written comment without selecting stars.

5. Verification audit trail
   - Verification history records APPROVED/REJECTED, reviewing administrator, date/time, and document.
   - Documents can be viewed or downloaded.

6. Staff ID validation
   - New Staff IDs must use UFH/123 format (UFH + exactly 3 digits) and cannot be duplicated.

7. Student number validation
   - New student numbers must contain exactly 9 digits and cannot be duplicated.

8. New-student complaint verification workflow
   - Pending/unapproved students' complaints remain visible to administrators.
   - Assignment and admin responses are blocked until the student is approved.
   - Admin complaints page links directly to Verifications.

9. Other account improvements
   - Signed-in users can change their password.
   - Forgot-password flow verifies email plus student number/Staff ID before resetting the password.
